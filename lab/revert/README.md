# Revert bundle, 2026-09-05

A complete copy of v1.html, v1.css and v1.js as they stood immediately before
the bath handoff was rebuilt around `bath-steam.webp`, the single asset that
carries the steam and the bath in one photograph with a real alpha channel.

The version saved here is the one that was measured clean: no visible band at
any scroll position across the handoff, no dead scroll anywhere on the page,
the day clock reading true, and the Warm Paper field under the two acts below.

To undo the rebuild, copy these three files back over the originals in the
folder above. The old assets they depend on, `ground-fog.webp` and
`bath-scene.webp`, are still in assets/img and were not deleted.
